Mailbox math

The parcel that "did not fit" cost you a trip to the depot, the letter with one stamp too few comes back a week later, and the mailbox nobody emptied tells the street you are away. Mailbox math checks every face of a parcel against your letterbox slot, turns letter weight into the stamps and the cost, and counts the days until an unemptied box overflows. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Slot plan - slot and parcel become a fit verdict and the face to use.
  • Stamp plan - letter weight becomes stamps and cost.
  • Fill plan - box volume and daily mail become days until full.

Labeled norms

Labeled slot: 25 x 4 cm letterbox. Labeled stamp: covers 20 g of letter. Labeled daily mail: 3 L arriving per day. These are reference values, not rules - your slot, your stamps and your mail decide the real numbers.
